Privacy Policy

The Last Alibi · Last updated: 6 November 2026

Summary

The Last Alibi does not ask for your name, email or any account, and it does not collect, sell or share personal information.

What is stored on your device

The game saves your settings (for example the detective name you type, your chosen style and room) and which cases you have solved. This is kept only in your browser's local storage on your own device. It is never sent to us. You can erase it at any time by clearing the site's data in your browser or uninstalling the app.

Third-party services

The game loads its fonts from Google Fonts. When it does, your browser connects to Google's servers, which may receive your IP address as part of a normal web request. See Google's Privacy Policy.

The game does not use analytics, advertising, tracking cookies or location data.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
